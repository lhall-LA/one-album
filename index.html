Choose your crate

Pick 8 to 10 albums you'd happily put on. They're your shelf for this run, like owning a few records. They stay fixed until the run ends.

    New run

    How this works.

    1. Each session gets one of three modes, at random: Shuffle, a random album from your library, or an album from your crate that you choose.
    2. Skip works in every mode. Albums play in order.
    3. Hold the button to end early. When it ends, nothing else starts. You pick the next one when you're ready.
    4. No re-rolls. If you don't fancy what you got, end it early. That's useful data too.
    5. Afterwards, one question: was that a good listen?
    6. A day or so later, a quick memory check: what did you listen to?
